      Technical Analysis & Placement Rationale

      1. Top Office Row & Corridor Propagation:

      Positioning the main router along the central hallway enables RF waves to easily pass into all four top offices (Office 1, Office 2, Office 3, Executive Suite) through interior drywall partitions.

      2. Kitchen & Security Office RF Mitigation:

      Microwaves emit 2.4 GHz noise, while DECT phones and 2-way radios in the security office cause channel congestion. Keeping wireless equipment away from both rooms avoids signal degradation.

      3. Conference Room Coverage & Perimeter Security:

      Extending coverage into the bottom-right Conference Room with an extender ensures high density client connectivity while insulated exterior walls keep outdoor signal leakage safe.
